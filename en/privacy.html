Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Who we are

ZapaKid is a product of Zapacit S.R.L., Str. Zambilei nr. 76, Ghimbav 507075, Brașov County, Romania, J2020002568087, VAT RO43395664 (“we”), the controller of the personal data described here under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). Contact: contact@zapakid.ro.

2. The zapakid.ro website

The website is only a presentation page. It has no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics and loads nothing from third parties. For security, the web server temporarily logs the IP address, time and page requested.

3. Your account in the app: Apple or Google only

A parent account is created only with Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. We have no passwords. Apple/Google give us a unique account identifier and, on first sign-in, your name and email address (Apple may give a relay address).

Age check: the app only asks the phone to confirm that the parent is at least 18; we never receive a date of birth. If the age can't be confirmed, no account is created.

Children don't create accounts: a child's phone works only after a parent scans its QR code and sets up the child's profile (first name, age, grade, languages).

4. Family chats and notes

Messages, attachments and notes are end-to-end encrypted on the phone (AES-256-GCM; the keys stay on the family's devices). Our servers store only encrypted data and cannot read it.

Translation happens on the phone when the system supports it. For languages the phone can't translate (for example Romanian), the message text is sent encrypted (HTTPS) through our server to Google for translation; we don't store it.

For notifications we use Apple (APNs) and Google services, which needs a notification identifier for the phone.

5. AI features (tutor, voice, photos, tests)

Your child's questions, photos they send (homework, Rubik's cube, timetable) and texts read aloud are sent through our server to Google Cloud (Vertex AI, Gemini) as our processor. We don't send names, email or other identifying data, and we don't store this content on our servers. Under Google Cloud's terms, Google doesn't use this data to train its models.

The tutor conversation history stays on the phone.

6. Where data is kept and who receives it

Data is stored on our own servers, run by our team, in Romania (EU). For fast and secure delivery of the website and the app (DNS, content delivery network, protection against attacks) we work with Cloudflare: traffic passes encrypted through Cloudflare's network, but account data and family content are stored only on our servers. Processors: Cloudflare (delivery and protection), Google (sign-in, AI, translation, notifications) and Apple (sign-in, age check, notifications). Where these partners process data outside the EU, transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR (the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses). We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

We don't sell data, show ads or build profiles.

7. How long we keep data

Account data is kept while the account exists. A parent can delete all family data at any time in the app (account 👤 → “Delete all family data”): messages, children's profiles and sessions are deleted immediately. Backups are deleted within 90 days.

8. Legal basis and children

We process data to provide the service you ask for (contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and for security (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)). Children's data is processed with the consent and under the control of a parent or legal guardian (Art. 8 GDPR).

9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Write to contact@zapakid.ro. You can complain to the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP, dataprotection.ro) or the authority in your country.

10. Changes

When we change this policy we update the date above; important changes are also announced in the app.
